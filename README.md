## Project Overview

Led a mixed-method consumer insights study to understand what drives retention among Gen Z and Millennial Snapchat users, and how brand positioning influences behavioral intent.
This project combined qualitative research with a 2×2 experimental design to test whether framing Snapchat as a messaging-first platform (vs entertainment-first) increases retention intent and ad receptiveness.

## Full Slide Deck:
### Research Question: How do Gen Z and Millennial users engage with Snapchat, what drives retention or abandonment, and can messaging-first positioning strengthen behavioral intent and monetization potential?

## Methodology
### Phase 1: Qualitative Research
- Conducted three 45-minute focus groups
- Ethnographic observation of user behavior
- Identified engagement and churn drivers
- Developed behavioral personas across age cohorts

Key qualitative insight:
Snapchat is used like a messaging app but marketed like an entertainment platform

### Phase 2: Experimental Design (2×2 Framing Test)
- N = 146 respondents (MTurk sample)
- Current users (n=116) and non-users (n=30)
- Random assignment to:
  - Messaging-first framing
  - Entertainment-first framing
    
Measured:
- 6-month retention intent
- Usage intent
- Openness to sponsored content
- Perceived value

### Key Findings
-  Messaging-First Framing Increases Retention
     - Messaging positioning increased 6-month retention intent (~21% uplift vs entertainment framing).
     - Effect strongest among Older Gen Z (19–24).
     - Messaging framing works as a retention lever — especially when targeted.

- Current Users Drive Ad Value
    - Framing does not significantly change ad willingness.
    - However: Current users show ~6.5× higher ad receptiveness than non-users.
    - Implication: Protecting current users protects ad revenue.

- Age Is a Stronger Driver Than Framing Alone
   - Regression analysis shows age cohort has 2–3× greater impact on intent than framing itself.
   - Messaging works best when aimed at Older Gen Z — not as a blanket repositioning.

### Strategic Implications
- Short Term: Adopt messaging-first positioning in brand and lifecycle campaigns targeting Older Gen Z.
- Mid Term: Protect high-value social assets (Streaks, Memories) to reduce churn.
- Long Term: Simplify UI for aging users to reduce friction and “teen app” perception.
